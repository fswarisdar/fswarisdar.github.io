Research

I study machine learning when data stay on many clients and cannot be pooled. Themes: who may update a shared model; how trust and contribution can be scored; how fairness across clients can be measured; and how collaborative training behaves under limited communication or unreliable updates. Application settings include privacy-constrained medical imaging and other organisational data silos.

PhD thesis: A Research on Reliable Personalized Federated Learning with Enhanced Fairness, Robustness and Collaboration (BJUT, 2025; advisors Prof. Zhen Yang; Dr. Yuwen Chen).
